From practice to performance

Make your music easy to find and play.

Whether you play solo or with a band, Songivo helps you prepare a working songbook for the way you perform. Explore the full feature overview or follow the documentation to get started.

Organize your songs

Bring your songs into a library, add tags, and search when a request comes up or rehearsal moves to another tune. Link related charts when you need to keep alternate parts together. Your personal library can sync between your devices.

Explore the song library guide

Use ChordPro charts and PDFs

Import the charts you already use. Work with ChordPro or text songs when you want to transpose, and bring in PDF sheets when you need the original page layout. Preview and adjust PDFs as you import them.

Read the ChordPro guide or learn about PDF workflows

Annotate as you rehearse

Mark an entrance, a change, or a reminder directly on a ChordPro chart or PDF. ChordPro annotations scale as the content size changes, and annotations on band songs remain private to you.

Learn about annotations

Build a setlist for the gig

Gather the songs for a show, reorder them as the running order takes shape, and add notes to individual slots. During the set, mark songs as played or skipped to keep track of where you are.

See how setlists work

Collaborate with your band

Create or join a band library for the songs and setlists you share. Keep it separate from your personal library, copy songs explicitly when needed, and use Gig Mode to push the current song to bandmates.

Explore band libraries

Stay focused in performance mode

Open your music full screen and move through the set as you play. Use auto-scroll, tap tempo, and the tuner when you need them, with performance controls close to the chart.

Explore performance mode
