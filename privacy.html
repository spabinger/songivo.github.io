Privacy policy draft

Privacy for Songivo.

Last updated: June 7, 2026. Replace the developer name and support email placeholders before publishing as the final App Store support policy.

Website

This website does not include analytics, advertising trackers, cookies, or third-party tracking scripts. GitHub Pages may process basic server logs as part of hosting the public website.

App data

Songivo stores your songs, PDFs, annotations, setlists, tags, settings, and band library data on your device. When sync is enabled, that data syncs through your Apple iCloud account using CloudKit.

No developer account backend

Songivo does not require a Songivo account and does not operate a separate developer-run account service for your song library.

Band sharing

When you create or join a band, shared band songs and setlists are exchanged through Apple CloudKit sharing. Band members with access to the shared band can see shared band content.

Microphone

The tuner uses microphone access for live pitch detection. Microphone audio is used for tuning feedback and is not stored by Songivo.

Support contact

If you contact support, the information you choose to send, such as your email address, message, screenshots, or diagnostics, is used to respond to your request.
