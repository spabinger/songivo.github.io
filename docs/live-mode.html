Performance

Live Performance

Live mode reduces distractions and keeps the most important controls close to the chart.

Use live mode

  1. Open a song or setlist slot.
  2. Enter full-screen performance mode when you are ready to play.
  3. Use the live bar for previous and next songs, tempo, scroll, and quick actions.
  4. Start, pause, or reset auto-scroll as needed.
  5. Exit live mode when you need editing or library management tools.

Performance controls

  • Auto-scroll uses a song-specific speed that can be adjusted during playback.
  • Tap tempo helps update timing without leaving the chart.
  • Slot notes appear during live playback when the setlist slot has notes.
  • Keyboard shortcuts support arrows, space, and escape where hardware keyboards are available.

Before a show

Open each critical chart once, confirm zoom and scroll speed, and check that setlist notes fit the way you expect.
