FAQ

Short answers to common Songivo setup, sync, library, pricing, and performance questions.

Do I need a Songivo account?

No. Songivo does not require a separate Songivo account for your personal library. Personal sync uses your Apple iCloud account.

What file types can I import?

Songivo supports ChordPro, plain text, PDF, image, and SongBook Pro files, plus Ultimate Guitar and E-Chords search results from inside the app.

Can I use Songivo without iCloud?

You can use local app data on the device. iCloud is needed for personal sync between devices and CloudKit-backed sharing workflows.

Are band libraries the same as My Library?

No. My Library and each band library are independent. Copy songs explicitly when you want material in another library.

Are my band annotations shared?

Band-song annotations are private per user. The shared song can be common while each player keeps personal markings.

Does the website track visitors?

The first Songivo website version does not include website analytics or tracking scripts.

What devices are supported?

Songivo is designed for iPhone and iPad on iOS/iPadOS 18 or later.

What is included for free?

The free tier includes 5 song additions and 5 active bands. Song additions are counted when songs are created or imported, and deleting songs later does not restore free additions.

How much does Songivo cost?

Unlimited song additions are available for 3.99€ per year or a 14.99€ lifetime unlock. Extra active band slots are €0.99 each, and each purchase adds one active band slot.

Can I restore purchases?

Yearly and lifetime purchases can be restored from the Purchases screen. Extra active band slots are repeatable purchases recorded from processed transactions.

Is there a band member limit?

Each band supports up to 25 invited members. The band leader is not counted as an invited member.
