Exporting

Exports let you share a single chart, a selected group of songs, or a full set outside Songivo.

Export options

  • Export the current song as PDF.
  • Export selected songs into one combined PDF.
  • Export setlist summaries as text.
  • Export a set overview or a full combined set PDF package.

Export a set

  1. Open the setlist.
  2. Choose the export action.
  3. Select summary, overview, or full set PDF depending on the target.
  4. Use the share sheet to save, send, print, or transfer the file.

Before sharing

Check copyright, CCLI, and performance-use requirements before distributing charts outside your own device or band workflow.
