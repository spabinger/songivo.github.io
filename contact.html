Support

Get help with Songivo

Use the documentation section to get help on common workflows. For individual support, email our support team with the details needed to reproduce the issue.

Email support

Send app questions, bug reports, and support details to our support team.

support@songivo.app

What to include

  • Your device model and iOS or iPadOS version.
  • The Songivo app version if available.
  • Whether the issue is in My Library or a band library.
  • For sync issues, include the visible sync status and any diagnostics the app asks you to share.
