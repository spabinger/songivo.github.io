Support

Get help with Songivo.

Use the documentation for common workflows. For direct support, email Songivo support with the details needed to reproduce the issue.

Email support

Send app questions, bug reports, and support details to Songivo support.

support@songivo.app

What to include

  • Your device model and iOS or iPadOS version.
  • The Songivo app version if available.
  • Whether the issue is in My Library or a band library.
  • For sync issues, include the visible sync status and any diagnostics the app asks you to share.
